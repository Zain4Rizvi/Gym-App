## Direction contract

THESIS: A notepad, then the diary that note becomes. The writing surface stays blank. The record is dates, names, and weights.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper, ink, hairline rules, system type for the note, serif for the diary date, one amber mark for the action. Rows divide by space and hairline.

STORY: Open the notes, write the session, leave the page, and read the sets on the dashboard.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Notes. Large title. Search. Each row is the first line, the date, and the next lines. A plus starts a note. The editor is only the text.

FORM: Notepad and training diary. Native type. No fitness-card grid.

FINISH: unreviewed.
